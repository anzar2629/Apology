My Dearest Shalu 🌷

Hey Shalu,

I know that we haven't really been talking for the past few days because of some misunderstandings between us. And yes, I genuinely accept that, in the beginning, the mistake was entirely mine. I have a habit of making decisions without thinking things through or understanding the situation properly, and I'm really sorry for that.

I'm sorry that because of my actions, you felt hurt and started questioning yourself, wondering what you had done wrong. I never wanted to make you feel that way, and I'm genuinely sorry if I made you doubt yourself.

I also understand that just saying sorry won't magically fix everything or undo what I made you feel. But I wanted to make something small for you, as a little surprise and a way of apologising. I know it might not be perfect or even that impressive, but honestly, I've put a lot of effort into making it.

And thank you, because even while trying to make an apology for you, I've ended up learning so many new things. You know what? This is actually my fifth website! The previous four attempts failed because of endless coding errors. But somehow, this one finally worked.

I guess your kindness is helping me improve my skills too. 😂

I don't even know how to describe how beautiful you are. This apology and this little surprise are for you, but somehow, my skills are the ones getting better!

And about Delhi, I know you've been saying that you won't talk to me until I come there. I promise that I'll do my best to clear my backlogs this semester, no matter what, and genuinely try to make it to Delhi.

And as for you coming to Hyderabad, I've already planned almost everything — where we'll eat, where we'll go, and all the places I'd love to show you. I'm just waiting for the day you finally visit.

But lastly, there's just one thing I want to ask: please don't hold on to the condition that you'll only talk to me once I reach Delhi. I'm going through a pretty difficult situation right now, and you know that too. You also have your exams and a really busy schedule, so I understand that things aren't easy on your side either.

I'm not asking you to forget everything or pretend nothing happened. I just don't want the distance between us to keep growing because of this.

Please, Shalu. Let's not let this misunderstanding take away the bond we share.

I'm genuinely sorry. ♡
